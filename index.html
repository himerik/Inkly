Inkly

Privacy Policy

What we don't do with your data, and why that's short to explain.

Effective date: April 9, 2026 · Last updated: April 9, 2026

In one sentence

Inkly stores everything on your device, makes no network requests, uses no analytics, and has no way to identify you — because it never sees you.

1. Who we are

Inkly is a mobile application developed and published by Ludojama, based in Georgia. This privacy policy explains how the Inkly application ("Inkly", "the app", "we") handles information when you use it. If you have any questions, you can reach us at himirik@gmail.com.

2. The short version

Inkly is a fully offline reading and drawing app. It does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information. There are no user accounts, no logins, no analytics, no advertising, and no trackers. The app does not communicate with any server owned by us or by any third party.

3. What information the app handles

Inkly works with the following data, all of which stays exclusively on your device:

  • EPUB files you import. When you open a book, the file is stored in your device's local browser storage (IndexedDB). It never leaves your device.
  • Drawings you make. Any drawings you create on top of book pages are saved locally alongside the book file, also in IndexedDB.
  • App settings. Preferences like theme, text size, and reading position are kept in your device's local storage.

None of this information is transmitted anywhere. We do not have a server. We do not have access to your books, drawings, or preferences.

4. Information we do not collect

To be explicit, Inkly does not collect any of the following:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or any contact information
  • Your location, IP address, or device identifiers
  • Usage statistics, crash reports, or telemetry
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Contents of your books, drawings, or notes
  • Any personally identifiable information of any kind

5. Third-party services

Inkly does not use any third-party services, SDKs, analytics tools, advertising networks, or external APIs. All code and resources needed to run the app (including fonts, libraries, and images) are bundled inside the app itself and loaded locally from your device.

The only external interaction possible is the system share sheet: if you choose to share a drawing using the "Share" function, your device's operating system (Android) handles the sharing, and Inkly is no longer involved once you pick a destination app. What happens after you share is governed by the destination app's own privacy policy.

6. Permissions

Inkly requests the minimum permissions required to function:

  • File access — needed only when you explicitly pick an EPUB file to open. Inkly uses the system file picker, which gives it access only to the specific file you selected. It cannot read any other files on your device.

Inkly does not request access to the internet, location, camera, microphone, contacts, calendar, or any other sensitive data.

7. Children's privacy

Inkly is suitable for all ages, including children under 13. Because the app collects no personal information whatsoever, it complies with the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the children-related provisions of the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR, sometimes referred to as "GDPR-K").

Parents and guardians can be assured that:

  • No information about the child is collected, stored remotely, or transmitted
  • No advertising is shown, targeted, or otherwise
  • No behavioral tracking or profiling takes place
  • No third-party services can receive any data about the child's use of Inkly

If you are a parent or guardian and have any concerns, please contact us at himirik@gmail.com.

8. Data security

Because all of your data stays on your device, its security depends on the security of your device itself. We recommend keeping your device's operating system up to date and using a screen lock. If you uninstall Inkly, all data it created (books, drawings, settings) is permanently deleted along with the app.

9. Your rights

Under data protection laws such as the EU GDPR and similar regulations elsewhere, you have rights including the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. Since Inkly does not collect or hold any personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, or delete on a server — the only data is on your own device and fully under your control. You can delete it at any time by removing books and drawings within the app, by clearing the app's storage in your device settings, or by uninstalling Inkly.

10. Changes to this policy

If the way Inkly handles data ever changes (for example, if a future version adds an optional cloud sync feature), this policy will be updated and the effective date at the top of this page will change. Material changes will be announced inside the app before they take effect. Continued use of Inkly after such changes means you accept the updated policy.

11. Jurisdiction

Inkly is published from Georgia. This privacy policy is governed by the laws of Georgia, without regard to conflict-of-law principles. To the extent that other privacy laws apply to you based on your location (for example, GDPR in the European Union, CCPA in California, or COPPA for children in the United States), we honor the rights those laws give you.
